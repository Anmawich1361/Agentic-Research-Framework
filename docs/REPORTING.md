# Reporting

## Reporting principle

The final report should be useful for the user's purpose, not just comprehensive.

## Templates

### Company Brief

Best for general company understanding.

### Meeting Prep Brief

Best for sales calls, interviews, partnerships, and vendor meetings.

### Industry Primer

Best for learning a sector.

### Investment Memo

Best for investment research and diligence-style work.

## Required report components

Every report should include:

- executive summary
- key findings
- risks
- open questions
- source appendix

## Citation behavior

Citations should come from evidence claims and source map entries.

A report should not invent citations or cite sources that were not captured during source discovery.
