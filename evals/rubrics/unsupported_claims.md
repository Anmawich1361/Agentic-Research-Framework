# Unsupported Claims Rubric

Checks whether broad claims are evidence-bound and appropriately caveated.

Pass criteria:
- No high-severity unsupported-claim issues unless the fixture explicitly expects them.
- Broad strategy, supplier, market, or advantage claims cite evidence or are framed as inference.
- Recent-development claims cite concrete source evidence.

Common failures:
- Generic claims such as "durable advantage" without a claim citation.
- Supplier-meeting advice stated as fact without direct support.
- Recent claims based only on source metadata.
