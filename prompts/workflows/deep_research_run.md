# Deep Research Workflow

Use this workflow after the checkpoint.

Inputs:

- research charter
- research plan
- approved or high-scoring source map
- user steering notes, if available

Steps:

1. Extract evidence claims.
2. Run relevant specialist analysis.
3. Add findings to the evidence ledger.
4. Identify contradictions and gaps.
5. Prepare synthesis notes.

Do not produce uncited final claims.
