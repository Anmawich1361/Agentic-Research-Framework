# Evidence Ledger Policy

## Purpose

The evidence ledger is the source of truth for report claims.

## Rules

- Every fact claim must have a source.
- Every high-confidence claim must be directly supported by an authoritative source.
- Inferences must be labeled as inferences.
- Opinion claims must identify whose opinion they are.
- Weak evidence should result in low or medium confidence.
- Unsupported claims should be removed or moved to open questions.

## Final report constraint

The final report should be generated from:

- research charter
- research plan
- source map
- evidence ledger
- selected template

The final report should not rely on hidden chain-of-thought or untracked context.
