# Citation Policy

## Core rule

Every material factual claim in a final report should be traceable to the evidence ledger or source map.

## Material factual claims include

- what a company does
- how it makes money
- who its customers are
- financial metrics
- market size
- growth rates
- competitor claims
- regulatory claims
- recent events
- litigation
- leadership changes
- risk statements tied to specific facts

## Claims that still need support

Even if they sound obvious, support claims about:

- market leadership
- category creation
- moat
- competitive advantage
- rapid growth
- profitability
- customer concentration
- regulatory exposure

## Citation behavior

The report should include a source appendix and inline source references where practical.

A source reference should include:

- source title
- publisher
- date if known
- URL or source id

## Do not

- invent citations
- cite sources not in the source map
- cite a weak source for a strong claim
- cite company marketing as neutral proof of market position
