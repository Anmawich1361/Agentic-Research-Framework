# User Checkpoint Policy

## Purpose

The checkpoint prevents the system from doing deep research in the wrong direction.

## When to checkpoint

Always checkpoint after source discovery and before full research unless the user explicitly requests a full autonomous run.

## Checkpoint contents

- research charter
- research questions
- strongest sources
- weak or biased sources
- source gaps
- early read
- recommended research angle
- questions for user steering

## Questions should be specific

Bad:

```text
What else do you want to know?
```

Good:

```text
Should this emphasize investment risk, sales talking points, or competitor positioning?
```
