# Source Quality Policy

## Preferred source hierarchy

1. Regulatory filings and government data
2. Company primary sources
3. Investor materials and earnings transcripts
4. Industry associations and reputable trade publications
5. Reputable news outlets
6. Analyst reports and expert commentary
7. Vendor whitepapers
8. Blogs, newsletters, and weak sources

## Bias rules

- Company websites are useful for products and positioning, but biased.
- Investor presentations are useful but management-framed.
- Vendor whitepapers are often biased and should not be used alone for market-size or competitor claims.
- News is useful for recent developments but can be incomplete.
- Blogs are context only unless the author is clearly authoritative.

## Recency rules

For current company research, prefer sources from the last 12-24 months.

Older sources may be used for:

- history
- founding story
- long-term industry structure
- background definitions

Do not use stale sources for current market size, leadership, financials, or regulatory claims.
