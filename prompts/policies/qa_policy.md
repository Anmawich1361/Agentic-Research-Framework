# QA Policy

## QA should block final report if

- there are unsupported material factual claims
- the source appendix is missing
- high-confidence claims rely on weak sources
- the report does not answer the research charter
- major risks are omitted
- current claims are based on stale sources

## QA should warn if

- source base is thin
- information is mostly company-authored
- competitor set is incomplete
- financials are unavailable
- private company data is limited
- news sources disagree

## QA output

The QA review should be structured and actionable.
